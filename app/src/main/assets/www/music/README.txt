Optional: your own battle music.

The game has built-in chiptune battle music that changes with each factory.
To use your own track instead, put an MP3 in this folder:

  battle.mp3        plays in every battle

Or give a factory its own track (it wins over battle.mp3):

  nature.mp3  water.mp3  fire.mp3  storm.mp3  frost.mp3
  metal.mp3   psychic.mp3  shadow.mp3
  directorate.mp3   (Inspector Halt and The Foreman, including the final gate)
  neutral.mp3       (Pass and play against a Fellowship leader)

Tracks loop, so pick music that loops cleanly. Only use music you made
or have the rights to use.
